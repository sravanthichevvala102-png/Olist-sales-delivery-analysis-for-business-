# Olist E-commerce Sales & Delivery Analysis

## Objective
Analyze Olist's e-commerce data to understand sales performance,
customer behavior, and delivery experience, and recommend actions
to improve revenue and customer satisfaction.

## Business Questions
1. How are revenue and orders trending month over month, and where are the dips?
2. Which product categories and states drive the most revenue?
3. Who are the top customers, and how many are repeat buyers?
4. Do late deliveries lead to lower review scores?
5. What should the business do to improve revenue and customer satisfaction?

## Tools Used
MySQL, Python (Pandas), Power BI, GitHub
